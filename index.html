Features

Built for melody

01

Create up to 16 simultaneous melodies, each with a powerful 16-step sequencer and euclidean rhythm generator

02

Shape the harmonic structure with a global pitch quantizer module

03

Each sequence can transpose another sequence in real time, output control change data, or even shift the global pitch quantizer

Up to 16 banks of dual-function midi control change knobs with detents enabling up to 512 total controls

About

Made by a musician

The Glowfish was created by Gabriel Wheaton, a violinist and composer who wanted a midi sequencer that was intuitive and simple without compromising on flexibility and power. Over the past two years, he learned embedded programming and PCB design to make this dream a reality.
